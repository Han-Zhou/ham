# Neuronpedia adds 7M+ neurons for Qwen3-32B, Gemma 4 31B and Llama 3.3 70B

Asked by HanZ on 2026-10-28.

Neuronpedia, an open platform for browsing neuron and feature explanations, announced that it now hosts over 7 million neurons across three large open-weight models: Qwen3-32B, Gemma 4 31B and Llama 3.3 70B. The pitch is an interpretability "encyclopedia" that lets researchers look up what individual neurons respond to without running the models themselves. Why it's cool: it turns neuron-level interp from a one-off project into a shared reference, so it's easier to compare models and reproduce findings.

## Details

- Announced in a post on X dated 2026-10-26. The post links to per-neuron pages for each model.
- Scale: "7M+ neurons" across the three models, per the post.
- Replies in the thread asked whether all three models share one explainer model for labels. That detail matters for comparing neurons across models, and the post did not answer it in the text we could read.

## Why it matters (speculative)

- A browsable catalogue lowers the cost of hypothesis-hunting. You can check whether a neuron is monosemantic before building an experiment on it.
- A commenter suggested a leaderboard for least polysemantic neurons. That is a fun idea, but it needs a shared metric and labelling method to be meaningful.
